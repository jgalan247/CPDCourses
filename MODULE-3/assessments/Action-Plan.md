### If Students Misuse AI

**My response will be:**
1. **Conversation first** - understand their thinking
2. **Educate** - clarify expectations and why they matter
3. **Consequence** - proportionate to school policy
4. **Follow-up** - check understanding and rebuild trust

**I will NOT:**
- Assume the worst
- Shame publicly
- Punish without teaching
- Over-rely on detection tools
