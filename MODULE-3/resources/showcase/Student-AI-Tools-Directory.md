## Research & Learning Tools

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)
**What it does:** Conversational AI for answers, explanations, brainstorming
**Best for:** Research, concept explanations, study assistance, brainstorming
**Age:** KS3+
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🔴 High (can write essays, homework)
**Cost:** Free (GPT-3.5) | Freemium (GPT-4)

**Classroom Guidance:**
- ✅ Good use: "Explain photosynthesis to me" → "Now quiz me"
- ❌ Bad use: "Write my essay on Macbeth"
- **Teaching point:** AI as tutor, not author
- **Citation required:** Yes

**Age-Specific Tips:**
- KS3: Heavily guided, supervised, specific tasks only
- KS4-5: More independence, but clear boundaries on assessed work

---

### Perplexity AI
**What it does:** AI search engine with source citations
**Best for:** Research starting points, finding sources
**Age:** KS3+
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🟢 Low (citations built-in encourage verification)
**Cost:** Free | Freemium (Pro)

**Classroom Guidance:**
- ✅ Good use: Research topics, find credible sources
- **Advantage:** Automatically provides sources
- **Teaching point:** Starting point for research, not endpoint
- **Citation required:** Yes (tool provides sources)

**Why it's better than ChatGPT for research:**
- Shows sources automatically
- Encourages verification
- More transparent

## Writing Assistants

### Grammarly
**What it does:** Grammar, spelling, style checking and suggestions
**Best for:** Proofreading, grammar improvement
**Age:** KS2+ (with guidance)
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🟡 Medium (can substantially rewrite)
**Cost:** Free (basic) | Freemium (Premium)

**Classroom Guidance:**
- ✅ Good use: Checking spelling/grammar after writing
- 🤔 Gray area: Style suggestions
- ❌ Bad use: AI rewriting entire essay
- **The Line:** Proofreading OK, substantial rewriting not OK (for most assignments)
- **Discuss with students:** Where's the boundary?

**Teaching Conversation:**
"Grammarly is like having a proofreader. You can use it to check errors. But if it rewrites your sentences, you're not developing your own voice. Use grammar check, but write in YOUR words."

---

### QuillBot
**What it does:** Paraphrasing, summarizing, grammar checking
**Best for:** Paraphrasing (when done ethically)
**Age:** KS4+
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🔴 High (often used to disguise plagiarism)
**Cost:** Free (limited) | Freemium (Premium)

**Classroom Guidance:**
- 🚨 **High misuse risk**
- Students often use to paraphrase plagiarized text
- ✅ Ethical use: Paraphrase YOUR OWN notes for clarity
- ❌ Unethical: Paraphrase someone else's work to hide plagiarism
- **Teaching point:** Paraphrasing tool ≠ plagiarism eraser
- **Citation required:** Yes, if paraphrasing source material

**Red Flag:** If student work is perfectly paraphrased from one source, suspect QuillBot misuse

## Creative AI Tools

### DALL-E / Midjourney / Stable Diffusion
**What it does:** AI image generation from text prompts
**Best for:** Concept art, inspiration, visual mockups
**Age:** KS2+ (with supervision)
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🟡 Medium (passing AI art as own)
**Cost:** Free (limited) | Freemium | Paid

**Classroom Guidance:**
- ✅ Good use: Inspiration, mood boards, concept exploration
- 🤔 Gray area: Using in final project with attribution
- ❌ Bad use: Submitting AI art as own original work
- **Citation required:** Always ("Created with DALL-E/Midjourney")
- **Discussion:** What is creativity? Is AI art "real" art?

**Art Teachers:** Great for exploring concepts, discussing creativity and authorship

## Study & Revision Tools

**What it does:** AI tutor for study sets, personalized quizzing
**Best for:** Revision, vocabulary, concept checking
**Age:** KS2+
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🟢 Low (supports learning)

**Classroom Guidance:**

- **Citation:** Not required for personal study

---

### Photomath / Mathway
**What it does:** Solves math problems, shows steps
**Best for:** Checking work, understanding methods
**Age:** KS3+
**Learning Value:** ⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🔴 High (can do homework)
**Cost:** Free (basic) | Freemium (Plus)

- ❌ Bad use: Copy answer without understanding

## AI Detection Tools (For Teachers)

### GPTZero
**What it does:** Detects AI-generated text
**Reliability:** ⭐⭐ (60-80% accurate)
**Misuse Risk:** 🔴 High (false positives common)
**Cost:** Free (limited) | Paid (Pro)

**Teacher Guidance:**
- **Use cautiously**
- High false positive rate (flags human work as AI)
- **Never use as sole evidence**
- Students can easily bypass
- **Better approach:** Assignment design, conversations

---

### Turnitin AI Detection
**What it does:** Detects AI writing within Turnitin
**Reliability:** ⭐⭐⭐
**Misuse Risk:** 🟡 Medium (false positives possible)
**Cost:** Institutional license

**Teacher Guidance:**
- More reliable than free tools
- Still not foolproof
- Use as ONE indicator, not proof
- Combine with other evidence

## Age-Appropriate Guidelines

### KS1-2 (Ages 5-11)
**Recommended:**
- Simple creative AI (with supervision)
- Voice assistants for information
- Text-to-speech for accessibility

**Not Recommended:**
- ChatGPT or similar (too advanced)
- Independent AI use

**Approach:** Exploratory, supervised, wonder-focused

---

### KS3 (Ages 11-14)
**Recommended:**
- Research assistants (guided)
- Study tools (Quizlet)
- Creative tools (Canva, image AI)
- Language learning (Duolingo)

**Use Cautiously:**
- ChatGPT (only for specific, supervised tasks)
- Writing assistants (basic grammar only)

**Approach:** Guided use, teach critical evaluation

---

### KS4 (Ages 14-16)
**Recommended:**
- All tools from KS3
- More sophisticated research use
- Advanced creative tools

**Use Cautiously:**
- Writing assistants (establish clear boundaries)
- Math solvers (for checking, not doing)

**Critical:** Exam board AI policies - know them!

**Approach:** Responsible independence with clear boundaries

---

### KS5 (Ages 16-18)
**Recommended:**
- Advanced research tools
- Academic writing support (ethical use)
- University preparation skills

**Use Cautiously:**
- All tools - academic integrity crucial
- Know university AI policies

**Approach:** Prepare for university/workplace AI literacy

## Subject-Specific Recommendations

**Useful:** Inspiration, concept exploration

## Quick Reference: Teaching Framework

### For ANY tool, teach students to ask:

**Before Use:**
1. Is this appropriate for this assignment?
2. What's my learning goal?
3. Will AI help me learn or bypass learning?

**During Use:**
4. Am I using AI as a tool or a replacement?
5. Do I understand what AI is telling me?
6. How do I verify this information?

**After Use:**
7. What did I learn?
8. Do I need to cite AI?
9. Can I explain/defend this work as my own?
