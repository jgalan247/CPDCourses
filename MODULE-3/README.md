# Module 3: Student-Facing AI Tools & Digital AI Literacy

**Duration:** 3 hours
**Audience:** Teachers (all levels, all subjects)
**Format:** Interactive workshop with hands-on practice
**Delivery Mode:** Face-to-face or online
**Prerequisite:** Module 1 or Module 2 (or basic AI familiarity)

## Module Overview

This intensive 3-hour workshop shifts focus from teachers using AI to **empowering students to use AI** ethically, critically, and effectively. Modules 1 and 2 equipped you with AI tools for teaching; Module 3 prepares you to guide students in an AI-enabled world.

As AI becomes ubiquitous, students need digital literacy skills to navigate, evaluate, and create with AI responsibly. This module provides practical strategies for integrating student-facing AI tools into learning while developing critical thinking and ethical awareness.

### What Makes This Workshop Different?

🎓 **Student-Centered** - Focus shifts from teacher tools to student empowerment
🧠 **Critical Thinking First** - AI literacy, not just AI use
⚖️ **Ethics at the Core** - Academic integrity and responsible AI use throughout
🎨 **Creative Applications** - Beyond ChatGPT to research, creation, and exploration tools
👶➡️👨‍🎓 **Age-Appropriate** - Strategies for KS2 through KS5
🛡️ **Practical Safeguarding** - Detecting AI misuse while encouraging appropriate use
🚀 **Future-Ready** - Preparing students for an AI-integrated world

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, participants will be able to:

1. **Teach** students to use AI tools ethically and effectively
2. **Design** lessons that incorporate student-facing AI appropriately
3. **Develop** students' critical AI literacy skills
4. **Detect** AI-generated student work and respond appropriately
5. **Create** classroom policies for responsible AI use
6. **Evaluate** age-appropriate AI tools for different year groups
7. **Guide** student research, creativity, and learning with AI
8. **Address** academic integrity while embracing AI opportunities

## Key Topics Covered

### 1. AI Literacy Foundation
- What students need to know about AI
- Critical evaluation of AI outputs
- Understanding AI limitations and biases
- Prompt engineering basics for students
- Verification and fact-checking skills

### 2. Student AI Tools
- Research and learning assistants
- Creative AI tools (writing, art, music)
- Study and revision aids
- Language learning tools
- Accessibility and SEND support tools
- Subject-specific AI applications

### 3. Ethical AI Use
- Academic integrity in an AI world
- Attribution and citation of AI assistance
- When AI use is appropriate vs. inappropriate
- Transparency and honesty
- Data privacy for students

### 4. Detecting AI-Generated Work
- Linguistic and stylistic markers
- AI detection tools (and their limitations)
- Conversation-based detection strategies
- Assignment design to discourage misuse
- Responding when AI misuse is suspected

### 5. Lesson Design with AI
- Integrating AI into learning objectives
- Scaffolding AI use for learning (not replacing it)
- Age-appropriate AI activities
- Assessment in an AI-enabled classroom
- Differentiation using AI tools

### 6. Classroom Policy
- Creating clear AI use guidelines
- Communicating policies to students and parents
- Balancing restriction with opportunity
- Aligning with school and exam board policies
- Teaching responsible digital citizenship

## Age-Appropriate Approaches

### KS1-2 (Ages 5-11)
- **Focus:** Introduction to AI concepts, safe exploration
- **Tools:** Simple AI image/creative tools, voice assistants
- **Approach:** Supervised, exploratory, wonder-focused
- **Ethics:** "AI is a helper, not a thinker for us"

### KS3 (Ages 11-14)
- **Focus:** AI literacy, critical thinking, ethical awareness
- **Tools:** Research assistants, creative tools, study aids
- **Approach:** Guided use with increasing independence
- **Ethics:** Understanding bias, verification, academic integrity

### KS4 (Ages 14-16)
- **Focus:** Sophisticated AI use, deep critical evaluation
- **Tools:** Research, project planning, exam preparation
- **Approach:** Responsible independence with clear boundaries
- **Ethics:** Exam board policies, attribution, personal learning

### KS5 (Ages 16-18)
- **Focus:** Advanced critical analysis, ethical reasoning
- **Tools:** Academic research, complex problem-solving
- **Approach:** University/workplace preparation
- **Ethics:** Academic integrity, citation, career readiness

## Current Context & Challenges

### The Reality
- Students are already using AI (whether we teach them or not)
- Many use it poorly: copying without understanding
- Some use it well: research, ideation, learning support
- Most lack critical AI literacy skills
- Academic integrity concerns are widespread

### Our Opportunity
- Teach responsible AI use rather than ban it
- Develop critical thinking alongside AI skills
- Prepare students for AI-integrated futures
- Design assessments that value learning over outputs
- Model ethical technology use

### The Tension
This workshop navigates the balance between:
- **Restriction** ←→ **Embracing opportunity**
- **Academic integrity** ←→ **Authentic learning with AI**
- **Detection** ←→ **Trust and teaching**
- **Safeguarding** ←→ **Empowerment**

There are no perfect answers, but informed, thoughtful approaches.
