Getting Started with AI

Learn the basics of using ChatGPT and other AI tools for teaching tasks

Why AI for Teachers?

As a teacher, you're constantly juggling lesson planning, resource creation, differentiation, assessment, and communication. AI tools like ChatGPT can help you work smarter, not harder, giving you more time to focus on what truly matters—your students.

💡 Key Insight

AI is not here to replace teachers—it's here to give you superpowers. Think of it as a tireless teaching assistant available 24/7.

Step 1: Access ChatGPT

  1. Go to chat.openai.com
  2. Create a free account (or log in if you have one)
  3. You're ready to start! The free version is powerful enough for most teaching tasks

Step 2: Write Effective Prompts

The secret to getting great results from AI is writing clear, specific prompts. Here's the formula:

❌ Vague Prompt

"Create a lesson plan about photosynthesis"

✅ Specific Prompt

"Create a 45-minute lesson plan on photosynthesis for Year 7 students (ages 11-12). Include a starter activity, main activity with visuals, and an exit ticket. Align with UK science curriculum."

📝 The Perfect Prompt Formula:

  • Role: "You are an experienced [subject] teacher..."
  • Task: "Create/Generate/Write..."
  • Context: "For [year group/age], [ability level]..."
  • Format: "In the format of [list/table/paragraph]..."
  • Constraints: "Keep it under [length], use [reading level]..."

Try It Yourself: Your First AI-Generated Resource

Time: 10 minutes

Task: Create an Icebreaker Question

Use ChatGPT to create a creative icebreaker question for your next class. Make it fun, relevant, and engaging!

Example Prompt:

"Create 3 creative icebreaker questions for a Year 9 English class. Make them fun, thought-provoking, and related to storytelling or literature. Avoid generic questions."

💡 Reflection: How does this icebreaker compare to ones you've used before? Could you use AI to generate more for future lessons?

Brisk AI: Quick Classroom Resources

Create instant resources while browsing the web

What is Brisk AI?

Brisk is a Chrome extension that gives you instant access to teacher-specific AI tools. It can turn any webpage into a quiz, differentiate text, create lesson plans, and more—all with just a few clicks.

🚀 How to Install Brisk AI:

  1. Open Chrome browser
  2. Visit briskteaching.com
  3. Click "Add to Chrome" (it's free!)
  4. Create an account
  5. You'll see the Brisk icon (✨) in your browser toolbar

5 Powerful Brisk Features:

📝

1. Article to Quiz

Turn any online article into a comprehension quiz in seconds

📚

2. Text Leveler

Adjust reading difficulty for different student abilities

🎯

3. Learning Objectives

Generate clear, curriculum-aligned learning objectives

✉️

4. Email Generator

Draft professional emails to parents and colleagues

📋

5. Presentation Generator

Create Google Slides presentations from topics or outlines
