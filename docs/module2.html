Creating Quizzes with Quizizz AI

Generate engaging, interactive quizzes in minutes

Why Quizizz AI?

Creating good quiz questions takes time. You need to write questions at the right difficulty level, include plausible distractors, and align with learning objectives. Quizizz AI does this in minutes, freeing you to focus on teaching.

💡 Key Benefit

Quizizz AI can generate a complete 15-question quiz from any topic, article, or document in under 2 minutes—complete with multiple-choice options, images, and difficulty levels.

Step 1: Access Quizizz AI

  1. Go to quizizz.com
  2. Create a free teacher account (or log in)
  3. Click "Create" in the top menu
  4. Select "Quiz" then choose "AI-Assisted"
  5. You're ready to generate your first quiz!

Step 2: Generate Your Quiz

Quizizz AI offers multiple ways to create quizzes:

📝 From a Topic

Simply enter a topic like "World War 2" or "Photosynthesis" and Quizizz generates relevant questions.

"Create a 10-question quiz on the water cycle for Year 5 students"

🔗 From a URL

Paste any article or webpage URL and Quizizz creates questions based on that content.

Perfect for news articles, educational websites, or reading comprehension

📄 From a Document

Upload a PDF, Word doc, or paste text and get instant questions on that content.

Great for textbook chapters, handouts, or your own notes

🎯 From Standards

Select curriculum standards and Quizizz creates aligned questions automatically.

Ensures curriculum alignment with minimal effort

Step 3: Customize Your Quiz

Once generated, you can:

  • Edit any question to make it more specific or challenging
  • Add images, audio, or video to make questions more engaging
  • Adjust difficulty levels to match your students' abilities
  • Reorder questions or remove any that don't fit
  • Set time limits, shuffle options, or enable power-ups for gamification

Generating Personalized Feedback

Provide meaningful feedback efficiently using AI assistance

The Feedback Challenge

We all know feedback is crucial for student learning. But writing personalized, constructive feedback for 30+ students on every assignment? That's dozens of hours each term. AI can help you provide better feedback in a fraction of the time.

❌ Generic Feedback

"Good work! Keep it up. Try to add more detail next time."

Too vague—students don't know what to improve

✅ Specific, AI-Assisted Feedback

"Your thesis is clear, but paragraph 2 lacks supporting evidence. Try adding a specific example from the text. Your conclusion effectively summarizes but could strengthen the connection to your opening argument."

Specific, actionable, personalized

How to Use AI for Feedback

📝 The Feedback Formula:

1. Provide Context to AI

"You are reviewing a Year 9 history essay on the causes of World War 1. The rubric criteria are: thesis statement, use of evidence, analysis depth, and writing clarity."

2. Paste Student Work

(Copy the student's essay, answer, or project description)

3. Request Specific Feedback

"Provide constructive feedback highlighting 2 strengths and 2 specific areas for improvement. Use encouraging language appropriate for 14-year-olds. Keep under 100 words."

⚠️ Important: Always Review AI Feedback

AI-generated feedback should be a starting point, not the final version. Always read, personalize, and ensure accuracy before sharing with students.
