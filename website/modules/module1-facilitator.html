📋 Pre-Workshop Preparation

You're not just presenting information - you're guiding teachers through a hands-on transformation. Your role: make them feel CONFIDENT and EXCITED about using AI!

Key Facilitation Principles

  1. Show, don't just tell - Live demonstrations are crucial
  2. Practice over theory - 60% hands-on time
  3. Celebrate small wins - Every success matters
  4. Create safety - "You can't break AI!"
  5. Focus on practical - Everything must be usable Monday morning

Technical Setup (1 Week Before)

  • ✅ Install Brisk AI on presentation computer
  • ✅ Create Magic School AI account for demonstrations
  • ✅ Test all tools on school wifi/network
  • ✅ Prepare backup: screen recordings of each demo
  • ✅ Test projector/screen sharing
  • ✅ Have mobile hotspot as internet backup

Materials Preparation

  • ✅ Print or prepare digital handouts
  • ✅ Set up sharing platform (Padlet, Jamboard, Google Doc)
  • ✅ Create sign-in sheet
  • ✅ Prepare name badges or tent cards
  • ✅ Test any videos or multimedia

Content Preparation

  • ✅ Read through entire session plan
  • ✅ Practice all demonstrations
  • ✅ Prepare 2-3 subject-specific examples for your audience
  • ✅ Create example resources in advance (to show if demos fail)
  • ✅ Review prompt templates
Send Email 1 Week Before with: workshop logistics, pre-workshop tasks (install Chrome, create Magic School account), what to bring (device, lesson idea), and pre-workshop survey link.

  • ❌ DON'T dive into technical details yet
Read the Room: If participants seem anxious, slow down and add reassurance. If they seem eager, pick up the pace. Address mixed groups: "Some of you are AI experts, some haven't used it - both are perfect!"

✏️ Activity-by-Activity Facilitation Guide

Activity 1: AI Icebreaker Challenge 10 minutes

0:10 - 0:20 (10 minutes total)

Speaker Script: Introduction (2 mins)

"We're going to jump straight in! No long lectures - just doing. In pairs, you'll use AI to create the most creative icebreaker question for a classroom. You'll have 5 minutes.

Ready? Find a partner - 1-2, 1-2, 1-2... [count off or let them choose]

[Show on screen]: THE CHALLENGE: Use AI to create the most CREATIVE icebreaker question for a Year 7 class (or your own year group). Time: 5 minutes. Aim: Creative, engaging, classroom-ready!"

Facilitation Steps

Step 1: Pair Up (30 seconds)

  • Count off: 1-2-1-2 or let them choose
  • Groups of 3 are fine if odd numbers

Step 2: Access AI (1 minute)

  • Direct everyone to ChatGPT.com
  • Help anyone who needs account creation
  • Show your screen with ChatGPT open

Step 3: Create! (5 minutes)

  • Pairs create their icebreaker question
  • Circulate and support
  • Encourage: "Be specific! Ask for creative, unusual questions!"

Step 4: Share (2.5 minutes)

  • Ask 4-5 pairs to share their question
  • Display on screen or read aloud
  • Quick comment on each: "I love how creative that is!" or "That's so specific!"

Step 5: Vote (30 seconds)

  • Quick vote: "Which would your students love most?"
  • Celebrate the winner
  • Emphasize: "Every single one of these is usable!"

Debrief Questions (1 min)

  • "Who'd used AI before?" (show of hands)
  • "What surprised you?"
  • Key message: "You just created a classroom resource in 5 minutes. That's what AI can do!"

Example Prompts to Show

Create a creative icebreaker question for a Year 7 class on their first day.
Make it fun, safe, and engaging.
Common Issues: "I don't have ChatGPT" → Partner with someone who does. "The output is boring" → Try being more specific: "unusual", "creative", "fun"

Transition Script

"You just used AI. That's the biggest step! Now let's make it practical for your teaching with Brisk AI..."

🔧 Troubleshooting & Difficult Situations

Resistant Participant

Signs: Arms crossed, not engaging, negative comments

Strategy:

  1. Acknowledge: "I hear your concern"
  2. Don't argue: "Try it and see what you think"
  3. Pair with enthusiastic person
  4. Individual check-in during break
  5. Ask: "What would make this useful for YOU?"

Remember: You won't convert everyone. That's OK.

Technical Meltdown

Scenario: Internet fails / tools don't work

Backup Plan:

  1. Stay calm: "Technology! Let's use our backup plan"
  2. Show pre-recorded demos OR pre-created examples
  3. Focus on concepts and principles
  4. Encourage practice at home
  5. Offer follow-up support session

Running Over Time

If 10+ minutes behind:

What to CUT:

  • ❌ Gallery walk → Quick shares instead
  • ❌ Extension activities
  • ❌ Some example demonstrations

What NOT to cut:

  • ✅ Hands-on practice time
  • ✅ Creation time (Activity 5)
  • ✅ Key demonstrations
  • ✅ Closing message

Common Questions & Answers

Q: "Will AI replace teachers?"

A: "No. AI replaces tasks, not relationships. Can AI comfort an upset child? Build confidence? Inspire? That's all you."

Q: "Is this ethical?"

A: "Great question! AI is a tool, like calculators or spell-check. We use it wisely. We still review, fact-check, and make decisions."

Q: "What about data privacy?"

A: "Important concern. Never input identifiable student data. Check your school's policy. Use AI for content creation, not sensitive information."

Q: "My school blocks everything. How can I use this?"

A: "Talk to your IT department. Many schools are creating AI policies. Show them the benefits. Offer to pilot it."

Q: "What if it generates wrong information?"

A: "Excellent critical thinking! AI can make mistakes. ALWAYS fact-check, especially in Science, Maths, History. You're the quality control."

Reading the Room

Signs of ENGAGEMENT:

  • ✅ Focused on screens during activities
  • ✅ Asking questions
  • ✅ Excited chatter
  • ✅ Volunteering to share
  • ✅ Helping each other

Action: Keep the pace, build on momentum

Signs of CONFUSION:

  • ⚠️ Blank stares
  • ⚠️ Not starting activities
  • ⚠️ Asking basic questions repeatedly
  • ⚠️ Clicking randomly

Action: Slow down, show again, provide step-by-step instructions, pair with confident partners

Signs of BOREDOM:

  • ⚠️ Checking phones
  • ⚠️ Side conversations
  • ⚠️ Rushing through without care

Action: Increase pace, add challenge, show advanced features, ask them to help others
