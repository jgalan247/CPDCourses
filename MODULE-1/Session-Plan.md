# Module 1: Session Plan
## AI Tools for Daily Tasks & Resource Creation

**Duration:** 3 hours (180 minutes)
**Format:** Interactive workshop
**Audience:** Teachers (all subjects, all levels)

---

## Session Overview

This session takes teachers on a journey from AI-curious to AI-confident. The workshop is designed to be highly interactive, with more time spent practicing than presenting. Teachers will leave with concrete resources they created themselves.

### Session Philosophy: "Show, Try, Create, Share"
- **Show:** Quick demonstrations (5-10 mins each)
- **Try:** Guided practice (10-15 mins each)
- **Create:** Independent creation (20-30 mins)
- **Share:** Celebrate and learn from each other (10-15 mins)

---

## Detailed Session Timeline

### SEGMENT 1: Welcome & Foundation (0:00 - 0:20) - 20 minutes

#### 0:00-0:05 | Welcome & Housekeeping (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Welcome participants warmly
- Explain workshop format: "This is a doing workshop, not a watching workshop"
- Cover housekeeping: breaks, facilities, parking
- Explain the "safe to experiment" environment

**Key Message:** "There are no silly questions, and you can't break AI!"

#### 0:05-0:10 | Learning Outcomes & Session Structure (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Display the session structure clearly
- Highlight the three main areas: Brisk AI, Magic School AI, Differentiation
- Show the end goal: "You'll create at least 3 resources today"
- Mention the tools are free (with paid upgrades available)

**Share the Slide:** Session structure with timings

#### 0:10-0:20 | Activity 1: AI Icebreaker Challenge (10 mins)
**Objective:** Get everyone using AI immediately, build confidence, have fun

**Instructions:**
1. Pair participants up (or groups of 3)
2. Give them the challenge: "Use ChatGPT or any AI to create the most creative icebreaker question for a Year 7 class"
3. Time limit: 5 minutes to create
4. Share 3-4 examples with the group
5. Vote on the most creative

**Facilitator Tips:**
- Have ChatGPT open and ready
- Show them how to enter a prompt if needed
- Celebrate creativity and humor
- Use this to gauge the room's AI experience level

**Debrief Questions:**
- How many of you have used AI before?
- What surprised you about what it created?
- Did anyone struggle? (Address immediately)

**Transition:** "You just used AI. That's the biggest step! Now let's make it practical for your teaching..."

### SEGMENT 2: Brisk AI Showcase (0:20 - 0:50) - 30 minutes

#### 0:20-0:25 | Introduction to Brisk AI (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Explain what Brisk is: "Chrome extension = AI in every tab"
- Show the installation process (live demo)
- Highlight: "It's designed FOR teachers BY teachers"
- Show the Brisk icon in your browser

**Key Features to Mention:**
- Works on any webpage
- Has 50+ teacher-specific tools
- Free tier is generous
- Updates regularly

**Installation Demo:**
1. Open Chrome Web Store
2. Search "Brisk Teaching"
3. Click "Add to Chrome"
4. Show the icon appearing
5. Quick tour of the interface

#### 0:25-0:35 | Brisk AI Live Demonstration (10 mins)
**Facilitator Actions - Demonstrate 5 Quick Wins:**

**Demo 1: Instant Quiz from Article (2 mins)**
- Open a BBC News article about science/current event
- Click Brisk icon
- Select "Create Quiz"
- Show the 10-question quiz generated
- Highlight: adjusting difficulty level

**Demo 2: Differentiate Text Reading Level (2 mins)**
- Take the same article
- Click Brisk > "Change Reading Level"
- Show it rewritten for KS2 level
- Then show it for A-Level
- Emphasize: "Same content, different access points"

**Demo 3: Presentation from Topic (2 mins)**
- Click Brisk > "Create Presentation"
- Enter: "Photosynthesis for Year 8"
- Show the Google Slides generated with 10+ slides
- Point out: images, diagrams, key questions

**Demo 4: Email Template (2 mins)**
- Click Brisk > "Email"
- Scenario: "Email to parents about upcoming field trip"
- Show the professional email generated
- Highlight: tone adjustment (formal/friendly)

**Demo 5: Learning Objectives (2 mins)**
- Click Brisk > "Create Learning Objectives"
- Topic: "Rivers and Erosion"
- Show SMART objectives aligned to curriculum
- Mention: curriculum standard selection

**Facilitator Emphasis:**
"That's 2 hours of work done in 10 minutes."

#### 0:35-0:50 | Activity 2: Brisk AI Quick Wins - Hands-On Practice (15 mins)
**Objective:** Every teacher creates 3 resources using Brisk

**Instructions:**
1. Everyone installs Brisk (help anyone struggling)
2. Complete the worksheet challenges:
   - Challenge 1: Create a quiz from any article
   - Challenge 2: Differentiate text for 2 different levels
   - Challenge 3: Create learning objectives for your next lesson
3. **Extension:** Create email templates, presentations, or discussion questions

**Facilitator Actions:**
- Circulate and provide support
- Screenshot good examples (with permission)
- Note questions for Q&A
- Keep energy high: "You're doing great!"

**Share Time (last 3 mins):**
- Ask 2-3 people to share one resource they created
- Highlight variety and quality
- "What will you use Monday morning?"

**Transition:** "Brisk is quick and powerful. Now let's look at Magic School AI - think of it as Brisk's big sibling..."

#### 1:20-1:30 | Activity 3: Magic School Deep Dive (10 mins)
**Objective:** Create one substantial resource using Magic School AI

**Instructions:**
1. Choose ONE tool to explore deeply:
   - **Option A:** Create a full lesson plan for next week
   - **Option B:** Create an assessment for upcoming unit

3. Adapt/personalize it for your context

**Facilitator Actions:**
- Help participants choose based on immediate needs
- Support anyone stuck on prompts

**Share Time (last 2 mins):**
- Quick poll: "Which tool did you use?"
- One person shares their creation
- "Save these! You'll use them!"

**Transition:** "You've got two powerful tools now. Let's focus on something crucial: differentiation..."

### SEGMENT 4: Differentiation Deep Dive (1:30 - 2:00) - 30 minutes

#### 1:30-1:35 | Why AI Excels at Differentiation (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Brief discussion: "What makes differentiation time-consuming?"
- Common answers: Creating multiple versions, research on conditions, individualizing
- Key message: "AI doesn't get tired. It can create 5 versions as easily as 1."

**Share Statistics:**
- Traditional: 1 lesson adapted for 3 levels = 90+ minutes
- With AI: Same task = 15-20 minutes
- This means: More time for actual teaching!

#### 1:35-1:50 | Differentiation Examples Showcase (15 mins)
**Facilitator Actions - Show Real Examples:**

**Example 1: Autism-Friendly Resources (3 mins)**
- Show original worksheet vs. AI-adapted version
- Highlight changes:
  - Visual supports added
  - Clear, literal language
  - Reduced sensory overload
  - Step-by-step breakdowns
  - Visual schedules

**Prompt Used:** "Adapt this worksheet for an autistic Year 6 student. Include visual supports, use literal language, reduce clutter, and provide clear step-by-step instructions."

**Example 2: Dyslexia-Friendly Resources (3 mins)**
- Show text-heavy resource transformed
- Highlight changes:
  - Dyslexia-friendly font
  - Increased spacing
  - Chunked text
  - Key words highlighted
  - Reduced cognitive load

**Prompt Used:** "Rewrite this text for a dyslexic learner. Use dyslexia-friendly formatting, break into chunks, highlight key terms, and simplify sentence structure."

**Example 3: EAL (English as Additional Language) Support (3 mins)**
- Show complex instructions simplified
- Highlight changes:
  - Simplified vocabulary
  - Visual glossary
  - Sentence stems provided
  - Translation prompts
  - Cultural context notes

**Prompt Used:** "Adapt this lesson for EAL students with intermediate English. Simplify vocabulary, add visual support, provide sentence stems, and explain cultural context."

**Example 4: ADHD-Friendly Resources (3 mins)**
- Show standard lesson vs. ADHD-adapted
- Highlight changes:
  - Shorter activity bursts
  - Clear timers
  - Movement breaks
  - Visual checklists
  - Gamification elements

**Prompt Used:** "Redesign this lesson for students with ADHD. Include shorter tasks, movement breaks, visual progress tracking, and clear time boundaries."

**Example 5: Challenge for Gifted & Talented (3 mins)**
- Show standard task vs. extended
- Highlight changes:
  - Open-ended questions
  - Cross-curricular links
  - Research extensions
  - Creative application
  - Higher-order thinking

**Prompt Used:** "Extend this task for gifted and talented students. Add open-ended challenges, cross-curricular links, and higher-order thinking opportunities."

**Facilitator Emphasis:**
"Notice: we're not lowering standards. We're providing access points."

#### 1:50-2:00 | Activity 4: Differentiation Workshop (10 mins)
**Objective:** Create differentiated versions of one resource

**Instructions:**
1. Choose a lesson/resource you teach
2. Use either Brisk or Magic School AI
3. Create differentiated versions for AT LEAST 2 of these groups:
   - Autistic learners
   - Dyslexic learners
   - EAL students
   - ADHD support
   - Gifted & Talented
   - Visual/hearing impairment
4. Use the prompt templates provided in handout

**Facilitator Actions:**
- Provide prompt templates on screen
- Circulate and support
- Take examples for sharing
- Encourage specificity in prompts

**Quick Share (last 2 mins):**
- Ask: "Who created something they'll actually use?"
- 1-2 people share their differentiated resources
- Emphasize the speed: "This usually takes hours!"

**Transition:** "Now you have the skills. Let's put it all together..."

### SEGMENT 5: Create Your Own Resources (2:10 - 2:40) - 30 minutes

#### 2:10-2:15 | The Creation Challenge (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Explain the final activity: "Create 1-3 resources you'll use THIS WEEK"
- Show the options:

  **Option 1: The Full Lesson**
  - Complete lesson plan for next week
  - Include differentiation for at least 2 groups
  - Include assessment

  **Option 2: The Resource Pack**
  - Create 3-5 related resources (worksheets, quiz, exit ticket, etc.)
  - For one topic/lesson
  - Differentiated versions

  **Option 3: The SEND Support Kit**
  - Choose one student or group
  - Create 3-5 adapted resources
  - Include visual supports, modified texts, etc.

  **Option 4: The Communication Suite**
  - Parent emails (positive news, concern, invitation)
  - Report comments for 3-5 students
  - Newsletter content

  **Option 5: The Assessment Package**
  - End of unit test
  - Rubric
  - Self-assessment checklist
  - Mark scheme

**Rules:**
- Use both Brisk AND Magic School AI
- Everything must be adapted to YOUR context
- Focus on quality over quantity
- This is FOR YOU - not for show

#### 2:15-2:35 | Creation Time - Activity 5 (20 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Play soft background music (optional)
- Circulate and provide individual support
- Take screenshots of great work (with permission)
- Encourage participants to help each other
- Give time warnings: 10 mins left, 5 mins left

**Support Prompts:**
If someone is stuck, suggest:
- "What's your next lesson about?"
- "Which student could really use support right now?"
- "What takes you the longest to create normally?"

**Extension for Early Finishers:**
- Create additional differentiated versions
- Explore other Magic School AI tools
- Start building a resource bank structure

#### 2:35-2:40 | Save & Organize (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Guide everyone to save their work
- Suggest organization methods:
  - Google Drive folders by subject/year
  - Digital notebook tools
  - School VLE/LMS
- Remind: "These are starting points, not final products"
- Emphasize: "You've just created hours of work in 30 minutes"

**Transition:** "Let's celebrate what we've created..."

---

### SEGMENT 6: Sharing & Next Steps (2:40 - 3:00) - 20 minutes

#### 2:40-2:50 | Showcase Gallery (10 mins)
**Facilitator Actions - Choose ONE format:**

**Format A: Gallery Walk (Face-to-Face)**
- Participants put resources on desks
- Everyone walks around for 5 mins
- Leave post-it note compliments
- Return and discuss

**Format B: Digital Sharing (Online or Hybrid)**
- Use Padlet/Jamboard
- Everyone shares 1 screenshot + brief description
- Group browses and adds reactions/comments

**Format C: Rapid Fire Shares**
- 5-6 volunteers quickly share (30 seconds each)
- What they made and why
- One thing that surprised them

**Facilitator Actions:**
- Celebrate the variety
- Point out creative approaches
- Highlight time savings
- Photograph the board/screen for later sharing

#### 2:50-2:55 | Key Takeaways & Ethics (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Recap the key learning:
  1. AI can handle routine tasks
  2. Differentiation becomes manageable
  3. Brisk = quick tools, Magic School = comprehensive
  4. You're still the expert - AI is the assistant

**Important Ethics & Cautions:**
- Always check AI output for accuracy
- Don't share student data with AI tools
- Adapt AI content to your class context
- Be transparent with students when using AI
- Remember: AI suggests, YOU decide

**Show the reminder slide:**
"AI is a tool, not a replacement. You are the teacher."

#### 2:55-3:00 | Action Planning & Close (5 mins)
**Facilitator Actions:**
- Hand out/show Action Plan template
- Everyone commits to ONE action:
  - "This week I will..."
  - "This month I will..."
- Share the follow-up resources:
  - Email: All materials and handouts
  - Community: Link to ongoing group
  - Support: How to get help

**Post-Workshop Evaluation:**
- Request completion of post-workshop survey
- 2-minute form: What worked? What next?

**Final Message:**
"You've taken the first step. Keep experimenting, keep learning, and most importantly - keep teaching amazingly!"

**Thank participants and open for final questions**

## Facilitator Quick Reference

### Time Management Tips
- Set visible timer for activities
- Give 2-minute and 1-minute warnings
- Be flexible: if an activity is working, extend it
- Have extension activities ready for fast finishers

### Energy Management
- Start with high energy (icebreaker)
- Alternate sitting and active learning
- Use music during creation time
- Celebrate small wins frequently

### Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Tech not working | Have backup demos recorded |
| Participant overwhelmed | Pair them with confident peer |
| "I don't get it" | Show, don't tell - do it together |
| Runs over time | Skip gallery walk, do quick shares |
| Finishes early | Have bonus tools to showcase |
| Internet fails | Use pre-downloaded examples |

### Differentiation for Participants
- Visual learners: Slides and handouts
- Kinesthetic: Hands-on time prioritized
- Auditory: Verbal explanations with demos
- Beginners: Pair with experienced
- Advanced: Extension challenges
