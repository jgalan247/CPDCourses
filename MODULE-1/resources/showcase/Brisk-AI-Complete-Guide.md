## Installation Guide

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Open Chrome Browser**
   - Must be Google Chrome (not Safari, Edge, Firefox)
   - Update Chrome if needed (Help → About Chrome)

2. **Visit Chrome Web Store**
   - Go to: chrome.google.com/webstore
   - OR search "Chrome Web Store" in Google

3. **Search for Brisk**
   - Search: "Brisk Teaching"
   - Look for the official Brisk icon (blue/green design)

4. **Install Extension**
   - Click "Add to Chrome"
   - Confirm by clicking "Add extension"
   - Wait 3-5 seconds for installation

5. **Verify Installation**
   - Look for Brisk icon in browser toolbar (top right)
   - Click the icon to see the Brisk interface
   - Sign up with your email

6. **Pin the Extension (Optional but Recommended)**
   - Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome
   - Find "Brisk Teaching"
   - Click the pin icon to keep it visible

**Installation Time:** 30 seconds
**Account Required:** Yes (free email signup)

## Top 20 Brisk Tools for Teachers

### 📝 Content Creation Tools

#### 1. Create Quiz
**What it does:** Generates multiple-choice and short-answer quizzes from any webpage content

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to an article, video, or content page
2. Click Brisk icon → "Create Quiz"
3. Adjust settings:
   - Number of questions (5-20)
   - Question types (multiple choice, true/false, short answer)
   - Difficulty level
4. Generate
5. Export to Google Forms, print, or copy

**Best for:** Formative assessment, homework, revision
**Time saved:** 15-30 minutes per quiz
**Example:** Turn a BBC Bitesize article into a 10-question quiz in 20 seconds

---

#### 2. Learning Objectives Generator
**What it does:** Creates SMART learning objectives for any topic

**How to use:**
1. Click Brisk icon (can be on any page)
2. Select "Learning Objectives"
3. Enter:
   - Topic
   - Year/grade level
   - Subject
   - Curriculum standards (optional)
4. Generate

**Output:** 3-5 SMART objectives (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

**Best for:** Lesson planning, curriculum mapping
**Time saved:** 10-15 minutes per lesson
**Example:** Generate Year 7 Science objectives for "States of Matter" in 15 seconds

---

**Result:** Complete lesson resource pack in 5 minutes

## Tips for Getting the Best from Brisk

### 1. Be Specific in Settings
- Instead of "Create quiz," specify: "10 multiple-choice questions, Year 9 level, medium difficulty"
- Better results with more specific instructions

### 2. Use Favorites
- Star your most-used tools
- Access them quickly from the Favorites section

### 3. Combine Tools
- Use multiple Brisk tools on the same content
- Example: Simplify text, THEN create quiz from simplified version

### 4. Review and Adapt
- AI is a starting point, not a final product
- Always add your personal touch
- Check for accuracy

### 5. Save Frequently
- Download or export immediately
- Don't rely on browser history

### 6. Experiment with Settings
- Try different difficulty levels
- Test various question types
- Adjust tone for communications

### 7. Use on Multiple Content Types
- Articles (obvious)
- YouTube videos (use transcript)
- Your own documents
- Curriculum sites

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Brisk only work in Chrome?**
A: Currently yes. It's a Chrome extension. Use Chrome browser for Brisk.

**Q: Can students use Brisk?**
A: Technically yes, but it's designed for teachers. School policy may vary on student AI use.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Brisk has education-focused privacy policies. Don't input sensitive student data. Check your school's data policy.

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: No - requires internet connection.

**Q: Can I use it on school computers?**
A: Depends on your IT department's extension policy. Check with IT.

**Q: Does it replace my LMS (like Google Classroom)?**
A: No - it complements it. Create resources in Brisk, then upload to your LMS.

**Q: Can I trust the content accuracy?**
A: Mostly yes, but ALWAYS fact-check. AI can make mistakes, especially on complex or specialized topics.

**Q: How often do they add features?**
A: Regularly! Brisk is actively developed with new tools added frequently.
