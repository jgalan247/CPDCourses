# Activity 5: Create Your Own Resources

**Duration:** 30 minutes (20 mins creation + 10 mins sharing)
**Format:** Independent creation with facilitator support
**Objective:** Create 1-3 high-quality resources for immediate classroom use

---

## Overview

The culminating activity! Participants apply everything they've learned to create resources they genuinely need for their teaching. This is NOT practice - these are real, usable materials for Monday morning.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, participants will have:
- Created 1-3 classroom-ready resources
- Applied both Brisk AI and Magic School AI appropriately
- Integrated differentiation into their resources
- Personalized AI outputs to their specific context
- Built confidence in independent AI use
- Developed a workflow for future resource creation

---

## The Big Idea

**This is YOUR time.**

After learning the tools and techniques, you now get dedicated time to create what YOU need. No following instructions, no specific tasks - just practical, purposeful creation.

---

## Materials Needed

### Essential
- Computer with internet
- Access to Brisk AI and Magic School AI
- Clear idea of what you want to create
- Lesson plans, curriculum docs (for reference)

### Provided

- Prompt template library (from earlier activities)

### Optional

## Activity Structure

### Part 1: The Creation Challenge (5 minutes)

**Facilitator Script:**
> "This is it - your time to create something you'll actually use. Not practice, not demonstration - REAL resources for YOUR classroom. You have 20 minutes. Choose what matters most to you. Ready? Let's create!"

**Show on screen:**
```
THE CREATION CHALLENGE

Create 1-3 resources you'll use THIS WEEK

Use: Brisk AI + Magic School AI
Include: Differentiation where appropriate
Focus on: Quality over quantity
Time: 20 minutes

What will you create?
```

### Part 2: Choose Your Project (Participants decide)

Options presented in worksheet below - participants choose their own direction

### Part 3: Creation Time (20 minutes)

**Facilitator Actions:**
- Play soft background music (optional)
- Circulate and provide individualized support
- Take photos/screenshots of impressive work (with permission)
- Give time warnings: 10 mins left, 5 mins left, 1 min left
- Create a positive, focused atmosphere
- Encourage participants to help each other

**Don't interrupt the flow** - this is deep work time

### Part 4: Save & Organize (2 minutes)

**Facilitator Actions:**
- Guide everyone to save their work properly
- Show organization strategies
- Ensure no one loses their creations
- Provide time to download/export

### Part 5: Showcase Gallery (8 minutes)

**Choose ONE format based on group/setting:**

**Option A: Physical Gallery Walk**
- Participants display work on desks/tables
- Everyone circulates (5 mins)
- Leave positive post-it notes
- Discuss highlights as group (3 mins)

**Option B: Digital Showcase**
- Use Padlet, Jamboard, or shared document
- Everyone uploads screenshot + description
- Browse and react (5 mins)
- Discuss highlights (3 mins)

**Option C: Rapid Fire Presentations**
- 6-8 volunteers share (45 seconds each)
- What they made
- How they'll use it
- Group applause

## Step 3: Create! (18 minutes)

**Now GO! Create your resources.**

### Tips for Effective Creation

**Quality over Quantity**
- Better to have 1 excellent resource than 3 mediocre ones
- Take time to review and adapt AI outputs
- Add your personal touch

**Use Both Tools Strategically**
- Brisk for: quick tasks from existing content
- Magic School for: comprehensive planning and assessment
- Don't be afraid to use both!

**Differentiate Thoughtfully**
- Use prompts from Activity 4
- Focus on 2-3 most common needs in your class
- Remember: access, not lowering standards

**Personalize Everything**
- Add your own examples
- Use your students' names (changed for privacy)
- Reflect your school context
- Include your teaching style

**Check Quality**
- Read everything AI generates
- Fact-check content
- Ensure age-appropriateness
- Remove anything generic

**Save As You Go**
- Don't wait until the end
- Save after each resource
- Organize immediately

## For Facilitators: Managing Creation Time

### Creating the Right Atmosphere

**Physical Environment:**
- Comfortable temperature
- Good lighting
- Minimal distractions
- Accessible facilitator support

**Emotional Environment:**
- Positive, encouraging
- Celebration of progress
- No judgment
- "Safe to experiment"

**Optional: Play Music**
- Soft instrumental background music
- Not too loud or distracting
- Classical, lo-fi, ambient work well
- Participants can use headphones if preferred

### Your Role During Creation Time

**First 5 minutes:**
- Quick check-in with each participant
- Ensure everyone has chosen a project
- Help anyone who's stuck deciding
- Provide direction: "Start with the lesson plan, then build from there"

**Middle 10 minutes:**
- Circulate systematically
- Provide individual support
- Don't interrupt focused work unnecessarily
- Take photos/screenshots (with permission)
- Note impressive examples for sharing
- Answer questions briefly

**Questions to ask quietly:**
- "How's it going?"
- "Can I see what you're creating?"
- "Will you use this?"
- "Need any help?"

**Final 5 minutes:**
- Reminder to save work
- Identify volunteers for sharing
- Keep energy positive
- Countdown: "5 minutes left... make sure you're saving!"

**Last 2 minutes:**
- "Everyone save now"
- "Finish your current task"
- Prepare for transition to sharing

### Time Warnings

Give clear time warnings:
- **"You have 15 minutes left"**
- **"10 minutes remaining - halfway there!"**
- **"5 minutes left - start wrapping up and saving"**
- **"2 minutes - save everything now"**
- **"1 minute - finish your current sentence and save"**

### Supporting Different Needs

**For Overwhelmed Participants:**
- Sit with them briefly
- Help choose a simple project: "Create one lesson plan. That's it."
- Provide step-by-step guidance
- Pair with confident peer

**For Racing Ahead:**
- Celebrate their success
- Suggest extensions or refinements
- Ask them to help struggling neighbors
- Challenge: "Make it even better"

**For Off-Task:**
- Gentle redirect: "How's your resource coming?"
- Suggest: "Pick the simplest option - one worksheet"
- Provide structure: "Create just one thing"

**For Perfectionist:**
- Encourage: "Done is better than perfect"
- "This is a starting point, not a masterpiece"
- "You can refine it later"

## Common Questions & Responses

**Q: "I don't know what to create"**
A: "What are you teaching next week? Create something for that lesson."

**Q: "Can I create something with a colleague?"**
A: "Absolutely! Collaboration is great."

**Q: "Should I do more quick resources or one big one?"**
A: "What do you need most? Trust your judgment."

**Q: "The AI output isn't quite right"**
A: "Perfect opportunity to practice adapting it! What would you change?"

**Q: "Is it okay if I don't finish everything?"**
A: "Of course! Quality over quantity. Even one resource is a success."

**Q: "Can I share this with my department?"**
A: "Yes! Check your school policy, but generally sharing is encouraged."

**Q: "I want to start over - is that okay?"**
A: "Absolutely. This is your time."

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|-----------|
| Participant is stuck | Sit with them, choose together, start first step with them |
| Can't decide which option | "Choose Option 1 - can't go wrong with a lesson plan" |
| Tool not working | Switch to the other AI tool, or facilitate troubleshooting |
| Finished too quickly | Great! Refine it, or start another, or help others |
| Overwhelmed by choices | Simplify: "One lesson plan. That's your only goal." |
| Perfectionism paralysis | "This is a draft. You can improve it later. Just create." |
| Technical issues | Pair with someone whose tech is working |
| Lost work | Check history in Magic School, or browser history |

## Gallery/Showcase Management

### Option A: Physical Gallery Walk (Face-to-Face)

**Setup (2 mins):**
1. Ask participants to display one resource on their desk
2. Provide post-it notes
3. Explain: "Walk around, look at others' work, leave positive comments"

**Gallery Time (5 mins):**
- Everyone circulates
- Leaves encouraging post-its
- Asks questions

**Debrief (3 mins):**
- Return to seats
- Ask: "What impressed you?"
- Highlight variety and creativity
- Take group photo of the resources

### Option B: Digital Showcase (Online or Hybrid)

**Setup (2 mins):**
1. Share Padlet/Jamboard link
2. Demo: Upload screenshot + brief description
3. "You have 3 minutes to upload"

**Browse Time (5 mins):**
- Everyone explores the board
- Adds reactions, comments, hearts
- Asks questions via comments

**Debrief (3 mins):**
- Screen share the board
- Highlight standout examples
- Discuss patterns and variety
- Save/export the board to share later

### Option C: Rapid Fire Presentations

**Setup (1 min):**
1. Ask for 6-8 volunteers
2. "You have 45 seconds each"
3. Show on screen if possible

**Presentations (6 mins):**
- Each person shares:
  - What they made
  - How they'll use it
  - One thing that surprised them
- Group applause after each
- Keep time strictly (use timer)

**Debrief (2 mins):**
- Thank sharers
- Quick poll: "Who created something they'll use THIS WEEK?"
- Celebrate the collective achievement

**Immediate Reaction:**
