# Activity 2: Brisk AI Quick Wins

**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Format:** Individual hands-on practice with facilitator support
**Objective:** Create 3 usable teaching resources using Brisk AI

---

## Overview

Participants install Brisk AI and immediately create practical resources they can use in their teaching. This activity demonstrates the speed and ease of browser-based AI tools.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, participants will be able to:
- Install and navigate the Brisk AI Chrome extension
- Create quizzes from any online content
- Differentiate text for different reading levels
- Generate curriculum-aligned learning objectives
- Identify 5+ ways Brisk can save time in daily teaching

## PARTICIPANT WORKSHEET: Brisk AI Quick Wins

### Your Mission
Complete at least 3 challenges in 10 minutes. Create resources you'll actually use!

---

### 🚀 CHALLENGE 1: Instant Quiz Creation (3 minutes)

**What you'll do:** Turn any online article into a quiz

**Steps:**
1. Find an article related to your subject:
   - Go to BBC News, National Geographic, or your curriculum site
   - OR use one of the suggested articles below
2. Click the **Brisk icon** in your browser toolbar
3. Select **"Create Quiz"** or **"Quiz from Selection"**
4. Adjust settings:
   - Number of questions (5-10)
   - Question types (multiple choice, short answer, etc.)
   - Difficulty level (adjust for your year group)
5. Click **Generate**
6. Review the quiz - download or copy to your platform

**✅ Success:** You have a ready-to-use quiz!

**💡 Tips:**
- Select specific text in the article first for focused quizzes
- Choose "Multiple choice" for faster marking
- You can regenerate if you don't like it

**Suggested Articles (if you need one):**
- BBC Bitesize (your subject area)
- Newsround (for younger students)
- National Geographic Kids
- Any curriculum content page

**Save your quiz!** You'll use it!

---

### 🚀 CHALLENGE 2: Differentiate Text (3 minutes)

**What you'll do:** Adapt the same article for different reading levels

**Steps:**
1. Stay on the same article from Challenge 1
2. Click the **Brisk icon**
3. Select **"Change Reading Level"** or **"Simplify Text"**
4. Choose a target:
   - **Option A:** Simplify for struggling readers (KS2 level)
   - **Option B:** Increase complexity for advanced readers (A-Level)
5. Click **Generate**
6. Compare: original vs. adapted
7. Download both versions

**✅ Success:** You have 2-3 versions of the same content!

**💡 Tips:**
- This is perfect for mixed-ability classes
- You can specify: "Reading age 8" or "Advanced academic"
- Works great for EAL students too

**Extra Challenge:**
Create 3 versions:
- Foundation level
- Standard level
- Extension level

---

### 🚀 CHALLENGE 3: Learning Objectives Generator (3 minutes)

**What you'll do:** Generate SMART learning objectives for your next lesson

**Steps:**
1. Click the **Brisk icon** (from any page - doesn't need content)
2. Select **"Create Learning Objectives"** or **"Learning Goals"**
3. Enter your lesson details:
   - Topic: (e.g., "The Water Cycle")
   - Year group: (e.g., "Year 5")
   - Subject: (e.g., "Science")
   - Duration: (e.g., "60 minutes")
4. Optional: Select curriculum standards
5. Click **Generate**
6. Review the objectives - they should be:
   - Specific
   - Measurable
   - Action-oriented
   - Curriculum-aligned
7. Copy to your lesson plan

**✅ Success:** You have SMART objectives ready to use!

**💡 Tips:**
- Be specific in your topic: "Photosynthesis - light reaction" vs. "Plants"
- Select your curriculum if available (UK National Curriculum, etc.)
- You can ask for differentiated objectives (All/Most/Some)

**Example Input:**
```
Topic: Rivers and erosion
Year: Year 7 Geography
Curriculum: UK National Curriculum
Duration: 50 minutes
```

### Circulation Strategy

**While participants work:**
1. **First 3 minutes:** Focus on people who look stuck
2. **Middle 6 minutes:** Check in with everyone, screenshot good work
3. **Last 3 minutes:** Identify people to share, prepare transition

**Questions to ask as you circulate:**
- "How's it going?"
- "Can I see what you've created?"
- "Will you use this?"
- "What else would you like to try?"

### Common Questions & Answers

**Q: "Do I need to pay for Brisk?"**
A: "Free version is generous. Most features are free. Premium unlocks unlimited usage."

**Q: "Will this work in Safari/Edge?"**
A: "Brisk is Chrome-only, but there are similar tools for other browsers. Chrome is recommended for today."

**Q: "Can I use this with my students?"**
A: "Absolutely! But remember: you're using it to create resources FOR students, not students using it directly (unless you choose)."

**Q: "Is this cheating?"**
A: "No more than using a photocopier or calculator. It's a tool. You're still the expert making decisions."

**Q: "What if it generates wrong information?"**
A: "Great question! Always review and fact-check. You're the quality control."

**Q: "This is amazing! Why haven't I heard of it before?"**
A: "It's relatively new, and not widely advertised. Share it with your colleagues!"

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Quick Fix |
|---------|-----------|
| Brisk icon doesn't appear | Refresh page, check extensions are enabled |
| "Generate" button greyed out | Select text first, or enter more details |
| Output is gibberish | Try different content, or regenerate |
| Quiz questions are too hard/easy | Use the difficulty slider, or specify in prompt |
| Can't save/download | Check pop-up blocker, try different save format |
| Participant is overwhelmed | Pair them with someone confident, do one challenge together |
| Internet is slow | Prioritize one challenge, skip others |
| Someone finishes in 3 minutes | Perfect! Direct to extension challenges |
