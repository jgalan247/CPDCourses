# Activity 3: Magic School AI Deep Dive

**Duration:** 10 minutes
**Format:** Individual hands-on exploration with choice
**Objective:** Create one comprehensive teaching resource using Magic School AI

---

## Overview

After experiencing Brisk's quick tools, participants now explore Magic School AI's more comprehensive features. This activity emphasizes depth over breadth - creating one high-quality resource rather than multiple quick wins.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, participants will be able to:
- Navigate the Magic School AI platform
- Create a comprehensive lesson plan, assessment, or differentiation tool
- Identify when to use Magic School AI vs. Brisk AI
- Adapt AI-generated content to their specific teaching context
- Save and organize resources from Magic School AI

---

## PARTICIPANT WORKSHEET: Magic School AI Deep Dive

### Your Mission
Choose ONE option below. Create something comprehensive and high-quality you'll use soon. Take your time and make it good!

**Time:** 7 minutes
**Goal:** One excellent resource, not multiple rushed ones

---

### 🎯 OPTION A: Create a Full Lesson Plan

**Choose this if:** You have a lesson to plan for next week

**What you'll create:** A comprehensive 45-60 minute lesson plan

**Steps:**
1. Go to Magic School AI dashboard
2. Find: **"Lesson Plan Generator"** (or search for it)
3. Enter your details:
   - **Topic:** Be specific! (e.g., "Photosynthesis - light reactions" not just "plants")
   - **Year/Grade:** Your students' year group
   - **Duration:** How long is your lesson?
   - **Learning Style:** Any preferences? (hands-on, discussion, etc.)
   - **Standards:** Select your curriculum if available
4. Click **"Generate"**
5. Review the output - it should include:
   - Learning objectives
   - Starter activity
   - Main teaching sequence
   - Differentiation suggestions
   - Plenary/review
   - Resources needed
   - Assessment opportunities
6. **IMPORTANT:** Adapt it to YOUR class:
   - Add your own examples
   - Adjust for your students' prior knowledge
   - Include your school's routines
   - Personalize language and tone
7. Save/download: Copy to Google Docs, Word, or your planning platform

**✅ Success Criteria:**
- You have a complete lesson ready to teach
- It includes differentiation
- You've personalized it
- It's saved where you can find it

**💡 Tips:**
- The more specific your input, the better the output
- Don't accept the first version - regenerate if needed
- This is a STARTING POINT - you're still the expert
- Add your personality and teaching style

---

### 🎯 OPTION B: Create an Assessment

**Choose this if:** You need a quiz, test, or assessment for an upcoming topic

**What you'll create:** A comprehensive assessment with mark scheme

**Steps:**
1. Go to Magic School AI dashboard
2. Find: **"Assessment Generator"** or **"Create Test"**
3. Select assessment type:
   - Formative (checking understanding)
   - Summative (end of unit test)
   - Diagnostic (pre-assessment)
4. Enter details:
   - **Topic/Unit:** What are you assessing?
   - **Year group:** Your students
   - **Question types:** Multiple choice, short answer, extended, practical?
   - **Number of questions:** 10-20 typical
   - **Difficulty:** Match to your students' level
5. Click **"Generate"**
6. Review the output - should include:
   - Clear questions
   - Mark scheme/rubric
   - Answer key
   - Marking guidance
7. **IMPORTANT:** Check for:
   - Curriculum alignment
   - Appropriate difficulty
   - No errors (AI can make mistakes!)
   - Cultural sensitivity
8. Adapt and personalize
9. Save/download

**✅ Success Criteria:**
- You have a ready-to-use assessment
- Mark scheme is clear
- Questions are appropriate for your students
- It's saved in a useful format

**💡 Tips:**
- Mix question types for deeper assessment
- Include extension questions for fast finishers
- Generate alternative versions for different abilities
- Always fact-check content!

---

### 🎯 OPTION C: Generate Accommodation Suggestions

**Choose this if:** You have a student who needs support (SEND, EAL, etc.)

**What you'll create:** Specific, actionable strategies for supporting a learner

**Steps:**
1. Go to Magic School AI dashboard
2. Find: **"Accommodation Suggestions"** or **"Differentiation Strategies"**
3. Describe the student situation:
   - **Challenge:** What's the student struggling with?
   - **Context:** Year group, subject, specific needs
   - **Examples:** Specific behaviors or challenges you've noticed
   - **Goal:** What do you want to achieve?

   **Example input:**
   ```
   Year 7 student with dyslexia. Struggles with reading-heavy texts
   in History. Becomes frustrated and disengaged. Excellent verbal
   skills. Needs strategies for accessing written content.
   ```

4. Click **"Generate"**
5. Review the output - should include:
   - 10-15 specific strategies
   - Classroom accommodations
   - Resource adaptations
   - Assessment modifications
   - Technology suggestions
6. **Select the most appropriate** strategies for YOUR student
7. **Optional:** Generate IEP goals if needed
8. Save and share with relevant staff

**✅ Success Criteria:**
- You have actionable strategies (not vague)
- They're specific to your student's needs
- You can implement them immediately
- They're saved for reference and sharing

**💡 Tips:**
- Be specific in your description
- Include what DOES work as well as challenges
- Generate for multiple scenarios if needed
- Share with TAs, SENCO, parents

---

### 🎯 OPTION D: Write Report Card Comments

**Choose this if:** Reports are coming up and you need inspiration!

**What you'll create:** Professional, balanced report comments for students

**Steps:**
1. Go to Magic School AI dashboard
2. Find: **"Report Card Comment Generator"** or **"Progress Report"**
3. For EACH student comment:
   - **Student context:**
     - Strengths
     - Areas for development
     - Specific examples/evidence
     - Personality/attitude
   - **Tone:** Positive, constructive, balanced
   - **Focus:** Academic progress, behavior, effort?
   - **Word count:** 50-100 words typical

   **Example input:**
   ```
   Year 9 Science student. Enthusiastic and participates well.
   Strong understanding of concepts but needs to add more detail
   in written work. Excellent practical skills. Sometimes rushes
   work. Positive attitude.
   ```

4. Click **"Generate"**
5. Review - should be:
   - Professional and appropriate
   - Balanced (strengths AND targets)
   - Specific (evidence-based)
   - Parent-friendly language
6. **IMPORTANT:** Personalize!
   - Add specific examples from YOUR experience
   - Adjust tone for the student/family
   - Remove generic phrases
7. Create for 3-5 students if time allows
8. Save in document or copy to report system

**✅ Success Criteria:**
- Comments sound professional
- They're specific to each student (not generic)
- They're balanced and constructive
- Parents would find them helpful

**💡 Tips:**
- Batch similar students for efficiency
- Adjust tone: encouraging vs. firm
- Available in multiple languages for EAL families
- Use the "regenerate" button for alternatives

---

### 🎯 OPTION E: Create a Resource Pack

**Choose this if:** You want multiple related resources for one topic

**What you'll create:** 3-4 complementary resources for one lesson/unit

**Steps:**
1. Choose your topic and year group
2. Create a combination of:
   - **Resource 1:** Lesson plan (use Lesson Plan Generator)
   - **Resource 2:** Worksheet or activity (use Worksheet Creator)
   - **Resource 3:** Assessment (use Exit Ticket or Quiz Generator)
   - **Resource 4:** Differentiation (use Simplify/Challenge tools)
3. Ensure all resources align:
   - Same topic and objectives
   - Progressive challenge level
   - Complementary activities
4. Save all in one folder:
   - Name clearly: "Year 7 - Rivers - Complete Pack"
   - Include: lesson plan, activities, assessment
5. Share with department if appropriate

**✅ Success Criteria:**
- 3-4 related resources created
- They work together as a coherent unit
- All are saved and organized
- You could teach from this immediately

**💡 Tips:**
- This is ambitious for 7 minutes - focus on quality
- Start with the lesson plan, then build around it
- Use Magic School's different tools together
- Great for sharing with colleagues

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: "Can I create multiple resources or stick to one?"**
A: "Stick to one for quality. If you finish early, start another."

**Q: "The output isn't quite right. Should I start over?"**
A: "No! Click regenerate, or edit what's there. AI gives starting points."

**Q: "Can I use this with students directly?"**
A: "You're using it to create resources FOR students. Whether students use AI themselves is a separate decision."

**Q: "Is Magic School free?"**
A: "Free tier is generous. Premium unlocks unlimited usage and extra features."

**Q: "This lesson plan is generic. Is that normal?"**
A: "Yes! That's why YOU adapt it. Add your examples, your class context, your personality."

**Q: "Can I share resources I create with colleagues?"**
A: "Absolutely! Check your school's policy, but generally yes."

| Problem | Solution |
