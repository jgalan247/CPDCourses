# Activity 1: AI Icebreaker Challenge

**Duration:** 10 minutes
**Format:** Pairs or small groups (2-3 people)
**Objective:** Build confidence with AI immediately, create fun classroom-ready content

---

## Overview

Get teachers using AI within the first 10 minutes of the workshop. This low-stakes, creative activity breaks the ice while demonstrating AI's practical classroom applications.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this activity, participants will:
- Use AI to generate creative content
- Understand basic prompt structure
- Feel confident experimenting with AI
- See immediate practical application for their teaching

---

## Materials Needed

- Computer/tablet with internet access
- Access to ChatGPT (free version works) or similar AI
- Optional: Shared screen for displaying results
- Voting mechanism (hands up, Mentimeter, etc.)

---

## Instructions for Facilitator

### Setup (Before Workshop)
1. Open ChatGPT or Claude in a browser tab
2. Test the example prompts below
3. Prepare a way to display participant responses (Padlet, Jamboard, or simple screen share)

### Introduction (2 minutes)

**Say:**
> "We're going to jump straight in! No long lectures - just doing. In pairs, you'll use AI to create the most creative icebreaker question for a classroom. You'll have 5 minutes. Ready?"

**Show on screen:**
```
THE CHALLENGE:
Use AI to create the most CREATIVE icebreaker question
for a Year 7 class (or your own year group).

Time: 5 minutes
Aim: Creative, engaging, classroom-ready!
```

### Activity Steps

**Step 1: Pair Up (30 seconds)**
- Count off: 1-2-1-2 or similar
- Let people choose if remote
- Groups of 3 are fine if odd numbers

**Step 2: Access AI (1 minute)**
- Direct everyone to ChatGPT.com or similar
- Help anyone who needs account creation
- Show your screen with ChatGPT open

**Step 3: Create! (5 minutes)**
- Pairs create their icebreaker question
- Encourage experimentation
- Circulate and support

**Step 4: Share (2.5 minutes)**
- Ask 4-5 pairs to share their question
- Display on screen
- Quick comment on each: "I love how creative that is!" or "That's so specific!"

**Step 5: Vote (30 seconds)**
- Quick vote: "Which would your students love most?"
- Celebrate the winner
- Emphasize: "Every single one of these is usable!"

**Step 6: Debrief (1 minute)**
- Ask: "Who'd used AI before?" (show of hands)
- Ask: "What surprised you?"
- Emphasize: "You just created a classroom resource in 5 minutes."

---

## Participant Instructions

### Your Challenge
Work with your partner to use AI to create the most creative icebreaker question for a Year 7 class (or adapt to your year group).

### How to Do It

**Step 1: Open AI Tool**
- Go to [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) (free version works)
- Or use Claude, Gemini, or any AI assistant you prefer

**Step 2: Enter Your Prompt**
Try one of these prompts (or create your own!):

**Basic Prompt:**
```
Create a creative icebreaker question for a Year 7 class on their first day.
Make it fun, safe, and engaging.
```

**Advanced Prompt:**
```
Create an unusual and creative icebreaker question for Year 7 students.
The question should:
- Be inclusive (no one feels left out)
- Spark interesting conversations
- Reveal something unique about each person
- Avoid common questions like "favorite color"
Give me 3 options.
```

**Subject-Specific Prompt:**
```
Create a creative icebreaker question for Year 9 Science class that relates
to science but is fun and non-intimidating. Include a follow-up activity idea.
```

**Step 3: Review and Adapt**
- Read what AI generated
- Choose the best one
- Adapt it to suit your students
- Add your own twist!

**Step 4: Be Ready to Share**
We'll hear from several pairs - be ready to share your favorite!

## Example Outputs (For Facilitator Reference)

### Example 1: Basic Response
**Prompt:** "Create a creative icebreaker question for Year 7."

**AI Output:**
"If you could have any animal's ability for a day, which would you choose and what would you do with it?"

**Why it works:** Visual, imaginative, no wrong answers

### Example 2: Advanced Response
**Prompt:** "Create 3 unusual icebreaker questions for Year 7, inclusive and conversation-starting."

**AI Output:**
1. "If you could add one subject to school that doesn't exist yet, what would you teach?"
2. "What's a skill you have that most people don't know about?"
3. "If you could time travel to any year in history for lunch, when and why?"

**Why it works:** Multiple options, prompts storytelling, reveals interests

### Example 3: Subject-Specific Response
**Prompt:** "Create a creative icebreaker for Year 9 Science class with a follow-up activity."

**AI Output:**
"If you were a scientist discovering a new species, what would it be and what would you name it? Draw or describe your creature."

**Why it works:** Subject-linked, creative outlet, engagement beyond talking

### Example 4: Differentiation-Aware Response
**Prompt:** "Create an inclusive icebreaker for mixed-ability Year 8 class, suitable for EAL and anxious students."

**AI Output:**
"Show us one object on your desk/in your bag that tells a story. You can show it without words, or tell us about it - your choice!"

**Why it works:** Visual option, no requirement to speak, personal control

## Extension Ideas

If pairs finish early, encourage them to:

1. **Create a series:** "Now create icebreakers for weeks 2, 3, and 4"
2. **Differentiate:** "Adapt your question for younger students (Year 3) or older (Year 12)"
3. **Make it subject-specific:** "Create the same idea but for your subject"
4. **Add an activity:** "What would students DO with these responses?"

---

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "I don't have a ChatGPT account" | Partner up with someone who does, or use teacher's demo |
| "It's not working" | Check internet connection, try refreshing, use facilitator's screen |
| "The output is boring" | Try being more specific in prompt: "unusual", "creative", "fun" |
| "I don't teach Year 7" | "Perfect! Adapt it to your year group" |
| Pair finishes in 2 minutes | "Great! Now create two more. Try different styles." |
| Someone is stuck on what to type | Show them one of the example prompts above |

## Differentiation for Participants

### For AI Beginners
- Provide exact prompt to copy
- Demonstrate on your screen first
- Pair with more experienced partner

### For AI Experienced
- Encourage advanced prompting
- Challenge: "Create without looking at examples"
- Ask them to help struggling neighbors

### For Anxious Participants
- Emphasize: "This is just practice, no wrong answers"
- Allow working individually if preferred
- Option to pass on sharing

---

## Debrief Discussion Points

After the activity, facilitators can use these prompts:

1. **Ease of use:** "Show of hands: Was that easier or harder than expected?"
2. **Quality:** "Would you actually use this in your classroom?"
3. **Speed:** "How long would it have taken you to create 3 icebreaker questions without AI?"
4. **Creativity:** "Did AI suggest something you wouldn't have thought of?"
5. **Limitations:** "Did anyone notice limitations or weird suggestions?"

## Facilitator Tips

### To Build Confidence
- Celebrate every contribution: "That's brilliant!"
- Name the expertise: "You're already prompt engineering!"
- Normalize struggle: "If it doesn't work first time, adjust and try again"

### To Manage Time
- Set visible timer
- Give 2-minute warning
- Be flexible: if energy is high, add 1-2 minutes

### To Create Energy
- Use enthusiastic tone
- Move around the room
- Play upbeat background music during creation time
- React positively to all shares

### To Handle Skepticism
- Acknowledge: "It's OK to be skeptical"
- Show evidence: "Look what we just created"
- Connect to workload: "Imagine having 10 more minutes in your day"

---

## Assessment Success Criteria

This activity is successful if participants:
- ✅ Actually use an AI tool (not just watch)
- ✅ Create at least one usable classroom resource
- ✅ Seem engaged and curious (not resistant)
- ✅ Ask follow-up questions
- ✅ Share with the group (even if not all)
